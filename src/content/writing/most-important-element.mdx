# The most important element in the world

When asking people "what is the most important element in the world", most of them will probably answer "Gold", "Diamonds". 
I believe it's none of these elements. In my opinion, the most important element in the world is uranium.

But why is that?

Uranium is the only element that fuels nuclear power. This is good and bad. But it ultimately shifted and re-arranged the power-structure of the world.
